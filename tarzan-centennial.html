About Artist Sal Amendola & The 2012 Centennial Project

Featured in ERBzine (#3844) and archived by Heritage Auctions, the Homeworld Press Centennial volume paired Edgar Rice Burroughs’ original 1912 All-Story Magazine text of Tarzan of the Apes and its 1913 sequel The Return of Tarzan with classic brush-and-ink illustrations by Sal Amendola, whose career at DC Comics (Detective Comics, World's Finest) and Archie Comics began in 1969 before he joined the faculty at New York’s School of Visual Arts.
