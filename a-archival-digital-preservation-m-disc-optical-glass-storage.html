Acid-free rag paper printed in the 16th century remains perfectly legible today, whereas a modern NAND flash SSD left unpowered in a drawer can suffer bit rot in under ten years. For independent publishers, comic historians, and personal library curators, long-term digital preservation requires physical phase changes rather than trapped electrons.

Inorganic Rock-Like Layers vs. NAND Flash

Unlike conventional optical discs that use organic dyes, M-DISC media etches data into an inorganic glassy carbon-metallic layer that cannot oxidize or fade under ambient light—giving home archivists true museum-grade permanence.
