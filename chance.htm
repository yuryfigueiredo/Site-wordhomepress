1. The Bishop Chance Adventures

Headlined by The Last Chance, Second Chance, Chance Encounter, Lost Chances, Random Chances, and Spirit Chance, James Gauthier’s signature adventure series revived the classic American tradition of heavily illustrated prose fiction.

2. The Magik Shoppe Series: A Pack of Trouble & A Pocket of Time

In A Pack of Trouble (262 pages, featuring 29 all-new black-and-white interior illustrations and a full-color cover by noted DC/Archie comic book artist Sal Amendola, plus a rare illustration by Barry Blair of Elfquest fame), two brothers enter a mysterious magic shop and purchase a pack of manga cards that plunges them across parallel fantasy realms.

3. The Earth Shock Trilogy (Earth Shock, After Shock & Deadly Shock)

Featuring cover and interior artwork by manga/comic pioneer Ben Dunn, the Earth Shock Trilogy blended high-stakes sci-fi catastrophe with kinetic comic-book visual storytelling.
