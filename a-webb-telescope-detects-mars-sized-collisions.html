Webb Detects Dust Halos Indicating Mars-Sized World Impacts

NASA’s recent observations of distant dust halos raise questions about the frequency of planetary smash‑ups in young systems.

BY Dr. Aris Thorne • Oct 31, 2026 • 4 MIN READ
