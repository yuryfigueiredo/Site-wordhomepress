The Legacy of Leonard Starr (1925–2015)

Launched in 1957 by the Chicago Tribune-New York News Syndicate, Leonard Starr’s On Stage (later titled Mary Perkins, On Stage) revolutionized photorealistic storytelling, theatrical lighting, and witty dialogue in daily newspapers—before Starr later became head writer of the iconic 1980s animated series ThunderCats.
